# The win: full-2, 2026-10-19 to 10-24

A fresh Survival world (Normal difficulty) and a fresh bot with an empty inventory, given one standing goal, its dream: "beat the game". No stage was loaded; the bot died along the way and carried on from where it respawned. The run is called full-2 and played on its own server on port 25592.

| Hours played | Milestone (the run's own clock, `gameProgress.milestones`) |
| --- | --- |
| 0.8 | Entered the Nether |
| 26.2 | Had its eyes of ender (blaze rods and ender pearls took 25 hours) |
| 27.0 | Located the stronghold |
| 76.1 | Entered the End (49 hours went on the End kit, deaths in the End and re-kitting) |
| 107.2 | Killed the dragon: the server logged `Jev has made the advancement [Free the End]` on 2026-10-24 at 06:53:40Z |

On the last of five trips to the End it came in with the dragon at 145 of 200 health and finished it with sword blows at the perched head, from holes it dug beside the fountain, ducking into roofed holes when the dragon charged, at 15 to 20 health.

**Where the 108.6 hours of the clock went**, largest first: getting back to the End after four deaths there, 23.0 hours; nights waited out sealed in, awake, with no bed carried, 17.1; climbing back to the surface, 4.8; iron armor and pickaxes, about 11; food, about 8; arrows, 3.

**Cost:** about 25,700 Jev decisions and 102 million input tokens up to the kill (the run's ledger totals, scaled to the 107 of its 133 hours played when counted), about $4.30 at Jev's list price of $0.042 per million input tokens; output is free.

**What it did not do** that a speedrun does: barter with piglins for pearls, or blow the dragon up with beds. Both are now on Jev's menu with the game's own numbers (notes 2106 to 2108).

The bot and server logs of the run are kept outside the repository: they hold coordinates, chat and replay paths.
